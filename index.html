Welcome to SET Days 2026!

Get ready for three days of learning, creativity, teamwork, and exciting activities. Join us as we explore the world of Science, Engineering, and Technology!

About SET Days 2026

SET Days 2026 is a proposed school event that celebrates Science, Engineering, and Technology. It aims to encourage students to explore different fields, discover new ideas, and develop their skills through educational and interactive activities.

The event will take place from December 2 to 4, 2026. Students will have the opportunity to participate in competitions, demonstrations, workshops, and other activities that promote creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving.

This event is designed for students, teachers, and other members of the school community who are interested in learning more about science and technology. Everyone is encouraged to participate, share ideas, and enjoy the different activities prepared for the celebration.

Activities and Events

Here are some of the proposed activities that students can participate in during SET Days 2026.

Robotics Challenge

Students will design, build, and test robots to complete different challenges.

Science Quiz Bee

Test your knowledge of science through a fun and competitive quiz.

Programming Competition

Participants will solve programming problems using their logical and coding skills.

Innovation Exhibit

Discover creative projects, inventions, and technological ideas made by students.

Poster Making Contest

Show your creativity by designing posters related to science and technology.

Technology Workshop

Learn new skills and gain knowledge through interactive technology demonstrations.

How to Join

  1. Read the event guidelines and available activities.
  2. Choose the activity you want to participate in.
  3. Register through the assigned organizing committee.
  4. Attend the event and enjoy the activities.
